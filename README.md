# A Walkable History of Art

**[Visit the museum](https://artmuseum.artfrompixels.com)** · [Watch the one-minute tour](demo/museum-demo.mp4)

A museum you can walk through in your browser. Travel along a timeline, choose
an artist, and step into a gallery of their work.

The aim is to make art history easier to explore: see who worked at the same
time, follow an artist's work through the years, and spend time with paintings
beyond the handful everyone recognises. Wikipedia's knowledge becomes a place
you can visit, freely, without an account.

## What you can do

- **Explore more than a thousand years of painting.** Browse 35 periods and
  traditions, including Chinese, Japanese, Indian, Persian and European art.
- **Find your own starting point.** Begin with Featured painters or choose
  All artists for the full collection of **526 artists and 82,139 works**.
  Switch between the Gallery Wall and Star Map, zoom into a period, or search
  for a name in Explore.
- **Walk through an artist's work.** Galleries bring together the works
  available from the sources, with no limit to famous paintings. Explore
  rooms arranged by date, with room styles and music chosen for each period.
- **Look closer.** Approach a painting, click it to inspect it, and read about
  the work or follow its source. Wall labels show recorded dimensions when
  available and whether the display is at original scale, reduced or enlarged.

On a computer, use **W A S D** to walk and the mouse to look; hold **W** for
three seconds to run. On a phone, drag to look and tap the floor to walk.
Click or tap a painting to inspect it. **Explore → How to explore** brings
back the short guide on the timeline.

The collection is a starting point, and coverage is uneven. Featured is an
editorial introduction to influential painters, with its
[selection explained here](docs/FEATURED_ARTISTS.md). The museum will keep
growing as more artists and works become available in the sources.

## Made possible by Wikipedia

Artist biographies, artwork information and images come from
**[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/), [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/)
and [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/)**. The museum brings
that material together; artist selection and timeline groupings are editorial
choices. Source links and image credits let you explore further.

Recorded sizes help convey scale, but very large or small works are adjusted
for the gallery. Some dates, dimensions and images are unavailable. This is an
educational way to explore the collection, with room for corrections and
better coverage.

The volunteers who write, photograph, catalogue and maintain this knowledge
make the museum possible. If you enjoy it, please
**[donate to Wikipedia](https://donate.wikimedia.org/)**. Free knowledge keeps
democracies strong.
